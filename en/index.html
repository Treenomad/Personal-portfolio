WORKING METHOD

From an ambiguous field problem to an operational system, the whole delivery path matters.

Industrial AI is not an isolated model. It is a system of problems, data, software, people and operational ownership.

I’m freid. I use this space to practice one thing continuously: understand hard industrial problems, then move technology into real use.

I care about what field users are solving, what existing systems allow, whether the evidence is sufficient and how a solution fits into actual work. Technology should serve those questions, not define them.

I am connecting industrial event analysis, knowledge systems and AI workflows into methods that can be discussed, tested, built and improved.
