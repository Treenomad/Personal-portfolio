Research framework

Reliable Industrial AI

Bringing data quality, error cost, human confirmation, control boundaries and feedback into one Industrial AI decision map.

Industrial AIReliabilityHuman-in-the-loop
Core question
Why can an accurate model still fail to enter real operations?
Context

Industrial AI rarely fails on algorithms alone. Biased sensing, operating drift, asymmetric error costs, unclear ownership and missing fallback paths can make a strong offline model unusable.

Approach
01

Define the decision first

Specify what decision changes, who confirms it and what a wrong action costs.

02

Define the evidence

Evaluate coverage, drift, explainability and signals that can be verified on site.

03

Design for operation

Add human review, fallback, feedback labels and monitoring before any closed-loop control.

Outcome

A review framework for predictive maintenance, anomaly detection and knowledge assistants—shifting the question from “can we model it?” to “can it create value safely?”

Public boundary
This is a research framework to be validated through reproducible experiments on public industrial datasets.
